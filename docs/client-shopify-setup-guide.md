# Mich Hair Luxury Wigs - Shopify Setup Guide

Hi! This guide will walk you through setting up your Shopify account so we can start building your online store.

**Time needed:** About 10-15 minutes

---

## Step 1: Create Your Shopify Account

1. Go to **[shopify.com](https://www.shopify.com)**
2. Click **"Start free trial"**
3. Enter your email address
4. Create a password
5. Enter your store name: **Mich Hair Luxury Wigs** (or similar)
   - Don't worry - this can be changed later
   - Your store URL will be something like: `mich-hair-luxury-wigs.myshopify.com`

**Note:** Shopify offers a free trial (no credit card required to start). After the trial, the Basic plan is $39/month.

---

## Step 2: Complete Basic Setup

Shopify will ask you a few questions. Here's what to select:

**"Are you already selling?"**
- Select: "I'm just starting"

**"What do you want to sell?"**
- Select: "Products" (physical goods)

**"Where would you like to sell?"**
- Select: "Online store"
- You can also add "Social media" if you want Instagram/Facebook shopping

**Business address:**
- Enter your business address (this is for shipping and taxes)

---

## Step 3: Skip the Theme Selection

Shopify will try to get you to pick a theme - **skip this for now**. I have a custom theme ready for your store that we'll install together.

Just click "Skip" or "I'll do this later" when prompted.

## Step 4: Give Me Access to Your Store

This is the most important step! I need collaborator access to build your store.

### How to add me as a collaborator:

1. In your Shopify admin, click **Settings** (bottom left corner)
2. Click **Users and permissions**
3. Scroll down to **"Collaborators"**
4. Click **"Send collaborator request"** (or look for my request if I've already sent one)

### OR - Send me a staff invite:

1. In **Settings** → **Users and permissions**
2. Click **"Add staff"**
3. Enter my email: **victor@omohasolutions.com**
4. Select these permissions:
   - ✅ **Themes** (Full access)
   - ✅ **Products** (Full access)
   - ✅ **Content** (Pages, blog, navigation)
   - ✅ **Online Store** (Full access)
   - ✅ **Settings** (Limited access is fine)
5. Click **Send invite**

I'll accept the invite and can start working on your store right away!
